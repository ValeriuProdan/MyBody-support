MyBody privacy policy

Updated 22 October 2026

MyBody stores your logs, imported health readings, workout routes and preferences on your device. No MyBody account is required. The developer does not receive your health records. MyBody has no advertising and the developer operates no analytics or crash-reporting service.

Apple Health and Health Connect access is optional. MyBody reads only the categories you authorize through your operating system, for your own logs and charts. You can change access in the system health settings. Bluetooth access is used to read devices you connect.

Optional Google Health sign-in communicates directly with Google to read the history you request. Google handles authentication; credentials remain in its native sign-in service. You can disconnect in MyBody and revoke access in your Google account. Google's sign-in SDK declares collection of account contact details, identifiers, coarse location and usage information for its functionality and analytics. Google's own privacy policy applies to its services.

Opening an activity route requests map tiles from OpenStreetMap. The map service receives normal network information, including your IP address and the map area requested. MyBody does not upload the recorded GPS track to that service.

Exports and backups are created only when you request them. They may contain sensitive health records. You choose where to save them and are responsible for sharing and deleting those copies. The chosen file or cloud provider's privacy policy applies. On iOS, MyBody excludes its local documents, including the health database and saved backups, from automatic iCloud Backup. Keep a manual backup if you need to move or recover your records.

Deleting a record hides it from your history and charts; deletion markers and some stored data may remain locally so an import does not recreate it. Removing the app removes its local storage according to your operating system's uninstall behavior. Copies you saved elsewhere remain until you remove them there.

Private-record access controls restrict visibility inside MyBody. Keep your device and backup files secure. Charts, scores and colour references are informational and are not a diagnosis or a medical device.

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support contact on MyBody's App Store listing.
